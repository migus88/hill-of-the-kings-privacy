**Version 1 · Last updated 14 October 2026**

This policy explains what data the game Hill Of The Kings ("the game") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what your rights are.

## 1. Who we are

The game is made by Yuri Sokolov, trading as Engine Room Games, Petah Tikva, Israel ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described here is used (the "controller").

Contact for anything about privacy: **privacy@engine-room.games**

## 2. The short version

- The game sends gameplay statistics, run decision logs and crash reports **only if you agree**. You can change your choice at any time under **Settings › Privacy**, and the game plays the same either way.
- This data is linked to a **random install ID**, not to your name, your Steam account or your hardware.
- We use the data to **balance the game and fix bugs**. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or build profiles of you.
- We keep it for **at most 6 months** (crash reports: at most 90 days).
- To delete your data, email us the install ID shown under Settings › Privacy.

## 3. What we collect and why

### 3.1 With your consent

The first time you start the game, it asks whether you agree to share data. Nothing below is sent unless you agree.

| Data | What it contains | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Install ID** | A random identifier created when you agree. It is not derived from your hardware, your Steam account or anything else about you. | To group data from the same installation, and to find your data when you ask us to delete it. |
| **Gameplay statistics** | Events such as sessions started and ended, runs started and finished, rounds, battles, cards played, rewards chosen, screens visited, buttons clicked and settings changed (for example volume, language, resolution and graphics quality), with the time, a session ID, the game version, the platform (for example Windows) and the policy version you agreed to. | To understand how the game is played, balance it and improve it. |
| **Country** | Your country, determined by our server from your connection. Nothing more precise is determined or stored. | To understand where players are, at country level. |
| **Run decision logs** | The starting seed of a run and the list of decisions you made in it (such as cards played and rewards chosen), with the game version, platform, outcome and timing. They contain **no video, audio or screenshots**. | To replay a run inside the game so we can reproduce bugs and check balance. |
| **Crash reports** | When the game crashes or hits an error: the error details and stack trace, technical information about your device (such as operating system, device model, processor, graphics card and memory), a recent excerpt of the game's log file, the current run decision log, the install ID and the game version. User names in file paths are replaced and your computer's name is removed before sending. | To find and fix crashes and bugs. |

**Legal basis (EU, UK and similar laws):** your consent. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings › Privacy. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

**When you turn sharing off,** the game stops sending data, deletes any data it has not sent yet, and stops crash reporting. If you agree again later, the game creates a new install ID, so new data is not linked to old data.

### 3.2 Without consent: game configuration

When the game starts, it downloads its configuration from our server (for example, switches that let us turn data collection off remotely, and the current version of this policy so the game can ask you again when it changes). This request contains only the game version and platform, **no install ID**.

As with any internet connection, our hosting provider sees your IP address while handling the request. It is used only to deliver the response and to protect the server from abuse (rate limiting), and **we do not store it**.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, secure and able to show you policy changes.

### 3.3 Steam features

If you play through Steam, the game unlocks Steam achievements, updates your Steam statistics and sets your Steam status, and Steam Cloud syncs your saves and settings (including your data sharing choice and install ID) between your computers. These are features of the Steam service you use, so they work regardless of the data sharing setting. Valve processes this data under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). The game does not send us your Steam ID or Steam name.

Purchases are handled entirely by Steam; we never see your payment details.

### 3.4 When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

### 3.5 What we do not collect

No accounts, names, email addresses (unless you email us), precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, screenshots, cookies, advertising IDs or hardware identifiers. We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell or "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not make automated decisions about you.

## 4. Who receives the data

We use these service providers. They process data only on our instructions, under data processing terms with us.

| Provider | Role | Data |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA) | Hosts our server, stores gameplay statistics, run decision logs and the event archive | Everything in 3.1 except crash reports; the IP address in 3.2 while handling requests |
| **Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry)** (USA; data stored in the EU) | Crash reporting | Crash reports |
| **Grafana Labs** (USA) | Dashboards we use to view gameplay statistics | Query results from gameplay statistics |
| **Valve Corporation** (USA) | Steam platform | As described in 3.3 |

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to a buyer or successor if the game or our business is transferred, under this same policy.

## 5. International transfers

Our stored data is held in Cloudflare's European region. Our service providers are based in the United States and may process data outside your country. Transfers from the EU, UK and Switzerland to the United States are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and their UK and Swiss equivalents) in our providers' data processing terms. We are based in Israel, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Gameplay statistics: event archive and run decision logs, with their index | At most **6 months**, then deleted automatically |
| Gameplay statistics: analytics database (Cloudflare Analytics Engine) | **3 months**, then deleted automatically |
| Crash reports | At most **90 days** (deleted by Sentry) |
| IP addresses | **Not stored** |
| Emails to us | As long as needed to handle your request |

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a copy of it;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it;
- **restrict** or **object to** its processing;
- **data portability**;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (Settings › Privacy);
- **not be discriminated against** for using these rights; the game works the same either way.

We grant these requests to everyone, wherever you live.

**How to make a request:** email **privacy@engine-room.games** with the **install ID** shown under Settings › Privacy. The install ID is the only thing that links data to you, so we cannot find your data without it. Note it before turning sharing off, because the game replaces it.

**What deletion removes:** your event archive, run decision logs and their index. Rows in the analytics database (Cloudflare Analytics Engine) include the install ID but cannot be deleted individually; they are deleted automatically within 3 months. Crash reports linked to your install ID are deleted from Sentry on request.

We answer within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows). If we decline a request, you can **appeal** by replying to our answer; we will respond in writing with our reasons. If your appeal is denied, US residents may contact their state attorney general. You may use an authorised agent; we may ask for proof of authorisation.

**Complaints:** you can also complain to your data protection authority, for example your EU member state's authority, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), the Swiss FDPIC, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner or South Africa's Information Regulator.

### US state privacy laws

In the last 12 months, the categories of personal information collected were: **identifiers** (the install ID), **internet or other electronic network activity** (gameplay statistics and run decision logs) and **geolocation** limited to country. They come from your use of the game, are used for the purposes in section 3, are disclosed to the service providers in section 4 and are kept as in section 6. We do not collect sensitive personal information. We have **not sold or shared** personal information, and do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling. Because we do not sell or share data, we do not act on opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

## 8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (up to 16 in parts of the EU), please ask a parent or guardian before turning on data sharing. If you believe a child has shared data with us, email us with the install ID and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), stored with providers that encrypt it at rest, and accessible only to us. Writes to our server require a key and are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so that very little about you exists to protect.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the version and date at the top. If the change affects what the game collects or how it is used, the game asks for your consent again.
